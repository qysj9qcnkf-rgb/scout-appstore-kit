Testing instructions
Install on your development store (any store with a product). A microphone is needed for step 5. 1) Install Scout. There is no plan to approve and no card to add: every store starts free with $10 of usage on us, and Home shows a "Started free" step with what is left. Picking a plan is optional (Settings, one click; on a development store it is a test charge, no money moves). 2) Home shows the setup steps; setup takes a minute or two. At the "Switch … on in your theme" step, click "Open the theme editor": the Scout app embed is already switched on; press Save. 3) Open Interviews and click "Turn it on (preview)". In preview only you see the banner, and codes are test codes. 4) Click "See the banner on your site →": the storefront opens with the interview banner (if your storefront is password-protected, its password is in Online Store › Preferences). 5) Back in Interviews, click "Try the call (test code) →", allow the microphone and talk with the interviewer for a few minutes; the call ends by itself once you have said enough. 6) Back in Interviews, click "Show 1 test call" above the list of calls: your call is there with its recording, its transcript and the test discount code. Customer data answers (the 15 questions)

1. Do you process the minimum personal data required?
Yes. Phone is never requested; addresses are cut to city/province/country; order lookups return only status, items and tracking.
2. Do you tell merchants what personal data you process and why?
Yes. In the privacy policy (https://scoutyourstore.com/privacy) and in the app's Interviews and Reach pages.
3. Do you limit use to that purpose?
Yes. No advertising, no selling, no training on store data.
4. Do you respect consent decisions?
Yes. By default invitations go only to customers whose Shopify email marketing state is SUBSCRIBED; unsubscribed, redacted and invalid are always skipped, and Klaviyo suppressions are honoured. A shopper starts a call only by choice, after being told it's recorded. The owner may also invite recent buyers and abandoned checkouts where the law allows it, never in the EU or UK.
5. Do you respect opt-outs of data sale?
Yes. We don't sell data.
6. Do you have retention periods?
Yes. Data stays while the store uses the app. It is deleted on customers/redact, and in full on shop/redact (48 h after uninstall). Event counts keep 30 days.
7. Do you encrypt data at rest and in transit?
In transit: yes (TLS everywhere: Shopify, Anthropic, Railway, Resend). At rest: yes (the relay's Railway volume, and Anthropic's storage of the conversations).
8. Do you encrypt backups?
Yes (Railway volume snapshots).
9. Do you separate test and production data?
Yes. Test stores and test calls are flagged and kept out of metrics.
10. Do you have a data loss prevention strategy?
Yes. Our written data protection policy covers it: secrets only on the server, the admin API returns them redacted, webhooks and sessions are signature-checked, TLS in transit, encrypted volume at rest, minimum data, retention and redaction, test data kept apart.
11. Do you limit staff access to personal data?
Yes. Our written access policy covers it: one person, the founder; each store owner signs in to their own store only; least privilege; access logged; offboarding the same day with key rotation.
12. Do you require strong passwords for staff?
Yes. Password manager and two-factor sign-in on every service that holds store data (hosting, AI provider, Shopify Partners).
13. Do you log access to personal data?
Yes. Every request for console data that names shoppers (conversations, transcripts, recordings, quotes, invite lists) is logged: the store, how the person signed in, a one-way session id, the endpoint, the time and the status; never the data or arguments; kept 30 days. Every tool call the owner's own AI app makes is logged (name and time, never arguments); privacy requests are logged.
14. Do you have a security incident response policy?
Yes. Our written incident response policy: who responds, containment, 72-hour notice to affected merchants and Shopify, review.
15. Do you use the data for any purpose beyond the app?
No.
